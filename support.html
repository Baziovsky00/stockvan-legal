Frequently asked questions

How do I add an item without a barcode?
Open a van, tap the "+" button, and fill in the item details manually — barcode scanning is optional.

How do teams work?
Invite teammates to your van from Settings. Everyone invited sees the same live stock list. The number of teammates you can invite depends on your plan.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions, or tap "Manage subscription" inside the app.

How do I delete my account and data?
Open the app → Settings → Delete account. This is permanent and cannot be undone from within the app; contact us within 30 days if you change your mind.
